A simple three-font system keeps the feed consistent without making every post identical.

Style direction

Soft lilac.
Dusty purple.
Editorial energy.

COLOR

One primary purple + one accent + soft neutrals. No random purple shades.

TYPE

Large headlines, short copy, clear hierarchy and generous negative space.

PHOTO

Real students, candid moments, movement and community — not stock-photo energy.

GRAPHICS

Small stickers, circles, arrows, hand-drawn details and subtle texture.

05 / Signature idea
